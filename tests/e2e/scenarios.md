## 1. Lobby — Room Creation (Happy Path)

### Scenario 1.1: Host Creates a Room
- **Given** Player 1 opens the app at `/`
- **When** Player 1 clicks the "Create Room" button
- **Then** A 6-character alphanumeric room code is displayed on screen
- **Then** Player 1 transitions to the Lobby waiting state
- **[AC-1]**

### Scenario 1.2: Host Sees Room Code Persists on Refresh
- **Given** Player 1 has created a room and can see the code
- **When** Player 1 refreshes the browser tab (or navigates away and back)
- **Then** The lobby UI still shows the waiting state (room code may be lost if state is not persisted — out of scope, but client should gracefully reconnect or show lobby)
- **[AC-1]**

## 2. Lobby — Joining a Room (Happy Path)

### Scenario 2.1: Guest Joins with Valid Code
- **Given** Player 1 has created room "ABC123"
- **When** Player 2 opens the app at `/`, enters "ABC123" in the join input, and clicks "Join"
- **Then** Player 2 transitions to the Lobby waiting state
- **Then** Both Player 1 and Player 2 see a "player joined" notification
- **Then** The match transitions to the Placement phase (auto or on trigger)

### Scenario 2.2: Join with Invalid Room Code
- **Given** No room with code "ZZZZZZ" exists
- **When** Player 2 enters "ZZZZZZ" and clicks "Join"
- **Then** An error message is displayed (e.g., "Room not found")
- **Then** Player 2 remains on the Lobby screen
- **[AC-2]**

### Scenario 2.3: Join a Full Room

- **When** Player 3 attempts to join with code "ABC123"

- **Then** Player 3 remains on the Lobby screen
- **[AC-2]**

## 3. Placement — Drag & Drop with Rotation (Happy Path)

### Scenario 3.1: Place All 5 Ships via Drag-and-Drop
- **Given** Both players are in the Placement phase
- **When** Player 1 drags each ship from the ship palette onto the own board grid
- **Then** Each ship snaps to valid cells on the board
- **Then** All 5 ships (Carrier 5, Battleship 4, Cruiser 3, Submarine 3, Destroyer 2) are visible on the own board
- **[AC-3]**

### Scenario 3.2: Rotate a Ship During Placement
- **Given** Player 1 is dragging a ship (e.g., Carrier) onto the board
- **When** Player 1 presses the `R` key while the ship is in drag
- **Then** The ship orientation toggles between horizontal and vertical
- **Then** The ship preview updates to reflect the new orientation
- **[AC-3]**

### Scenario 3.3: Drag Ship to New Position After Placement
- **Given** Player 1 has placed the Carrier horizontally at a1–a5
- **When** Player 1 drags the Carrier to a new position (e.g., c3–c7)
- **Then** The Carrier moves to the new cells; old cells become empty
- **[AC-3]**

## 5. Placement — Validation (Negative Cases)

### Scenario 5.1: Placement Rejects Overlapping Ships
- **Given** Player 1 places the Carrier at a1–a5 (horizontal)
- **When** Player 1 attempts to place the Battleship at a3–a6 (horizontal, overlapping a3–a5)
- **Then** The placement is rejected with an error message
- **Then** The Battleship returns to the palette or its previous valid position
- **[AC-5]**

### Scenario 5.2: Placement Rejects Out-of-Bounds Ship
- **Given** Player 1 attempts to place the Carrier at h1–h5 horizontally (col h=7, so 7–11; col 11 is out of bounds)
- **Alternate Given** Player 1 attempts to place the Destroyer at a9–a10 vertically (rows 9–10 is valid; row 10 is boundary)
- **When** The placement would extend beyond column `j` (index 9) or row `10` (index 9)
- **Then** The placement is rejected with an error message
- **Then** The ship snaps back to the palette
- **[AC-5]**

### Scenario 5.3: Placement Rejects Diagonal Ship
- **Given** Player 1 attempts to place the Cruiser diagonally from a1 to c3
- **When** The placement is submitted (or the drag is released)
- **Then** The placement is rejected; only orthogonal placements are accepted
- **[AC-5]**

### Scenario 5.4: Cannot Click "Ready" Without All 5 Ships
- **Given** Player 1 has placed only 3 ships on the board
- **When** Player 1 clicks the "Ready" button
- **Then** An error message is shown (e.g., "Place all 5 ships before ready")
- **Then** Player 1 remains in the Placement phase
- **[AC-5]**

## 8. Battle — Shooting Mechanics (Happy Path)

### Scenario 8.1: Hit Registers on Both Clients
- **Given** It is Player 1's turn; Player 2 has a ship at b3
- **When** Player 1 clicks cell b3 on their tracking board
- **Then** Within 500 ms, both clients update:
  - Player 1's tracking board shows a red `V` at b3
  - Player 2's own board shows a red `V` at b3
- **Then** The turn passes to Player 2
- **[AC-8][AC-9]**

### Scenario 8.2: Miss Registers on Both Clients
- **Given** It is Player 1's turn; no ship occupies cell d7 on Player 2's board
- **When** Player 1 clicks cell d7 on their tracking board
- **Then** Within 500 ms, both clients update:
  - Player 1's tracking board shows a gray `x` at d7
  - Player 2's own board shows a gray `x` at d7
- **Then** The turn passes to Player 2
- **[AC-8][AC-9]**

### Scenario 8.3: Sunk Ship Highlights Distinctly
- **Given** Player 2's Destroyer (length 2) occupies cells e1 and e2; e1 has already been hit
- **And** It is Player 1's turn
- **When** Player 1 clicks cell e2 on their tracking board
- **Then** The result is `sunk`; the `sunkShip` is "Destroyer"
- **Then** Both clients highlight the entire Destroyer (e1–e2) with:
  - Dark red background
  - Distinct border
  - Crossed-out `V` per cell
  - Sunk-reveal animation
- **Then** The turn passes to Player 2
- **[AC-8][AC-9]**

### Scenario 8.4: Turn Passes Regardless of Hit or Miss
- **Given** It is Player 1's turn
- **When** Player 1 shoots and scores a hit
- **Then** The turn passes to Player 2 immediately (no bonus turn)
- **When** It is now Player 2's turn and Player 2 shoots a miss
- **Then** The turn passes back to Player 1
- **[AC-7]**

## 9. Battle — Re-Shoot Rejection (Negative Case)

### Scenario 9.1: Server Rejects Shot at Previously Targeted Cell
- **Given** Player 1 previously shot at cell c5 (hit or miss)
- **When** Player 1 attempts to shoot cell c5 again on a subsequent turn
- **Then** The server rejects the shot with an error (code e.g., `CELL_ALREADY_SHOT`)
- **Then** The turn does NOT pass to Player 2
- **Then** Player 1 must select a different cell
- **[AC-10]**

### Scenario 9.2: Server Rejects Shot When Not Player's Turn
- **Given** It is Player 1's turn
- **When** Player 2 attempts to shoot at any cell
- **Then** The server rejects the shot with an error (code e.g., `NOT_YOUR_TURN`)
- **Then** The board state does not change
- **[AC-10]**

### Scenario 9.3: Server Rejects Shot During Non-Battle Phase
- **Given** Both players are still in Placement phase
- **When** Either player attempts to shoot at a cell
- **Then** The server rejects with an error (phase guard violation)
- **[AC-10]**

## 10. Game Over — Win Condition (Happy Path)

### Scenario 10.1: Player Wins by Sinking All 17 Opponent Cells
- **Given** Player 1 has hit 16 of Player 2's 17 ship cells
- **And** It is Player 1's turn
- **When** Player 1 shoots at the final remaining opponent ship cell and scores a hit
- **Then** The server emits a `gameOver` event with:
  - `winner`: Player 1's playerId
  - `reason`: `"allSunk"`
- **Then** Both players' boards are fully revealed
- **Then** A Game Over modal appears announcing the winner
- **Then** A "Play Again" button is visible
- **[AC-11]**

### Scenario 10.2: Game Over Modal Shows Correct Winner
- **Given** The `gameOver` event has fired with Player 2 as the winner
- **When** Both players view the modal
- **Then** The modal displays "Player 2 Wins!" or equivalent
- **Then** Both boards are visible in their final state
- **[AC-11]**

## 11. Disconnect — Mid-Battle Reconnect (Happy Path)

### Scenario 11.1: Reconnect Within 30-Second Grace Window
- **Given** A battle is in progress between Player 1 and Player 2
- **When** Player 2's browser tab is closed (simulating disconnect)
- **Then** Player 1 sees a "Player 2 disconnected" notification
- **Then** A 30-second countdown timer is displayed on Player 1's screen
- **When** Player 2 reopens the app, enters the same room code, and the client sends `requestReconnect` with the same playerId
- **And** This happens within 30 seconds of the disconnect
- **Then** The server emits `reconnectResult` with `success: true` and full `GameState`
- **Then** Player 2's UI rebuilds to the current Battle state
- **Then** Player 1 sees "Player 2 reconnected"
- **Then** The match resumes from where it left off
- **[AC-12]**

### Scenario 11.2: Forfeit After 30-Second Grace Window Expires
- **Given** A battle is in progress between Player 1 and Player 2
- **When** Player 2 disconnects
- **And** 30 seconds elapse without Player 2 reconnecting
- **Then** The server declares Player 1 the winner by forfeit
- **Then** A `gameOver` event is emitted with:
  - `winner`: Player 1's playerId
  - `reason`: `"forfeit"`
- **Then** Player 1 sees the Game Over modal with "Play Again"
- **[AC-12]**

### Scenario 11.3: Disconnect During Placement Drops the Room
- **Given** Player 1 and Player 2 are in the Placement phase
- **When** Player 1 disconnects
- **Then** The room is dropped immediately (no grace window)
- **Then** Player 2 receives a `lobbyNotice` with a message like "Opponent left during placement; returning to lobby"
- **Then** Player 2 is returned to the Lobby screen
- **[AC-12]**

## 12. Play Again — Rematch (Happy Path)

### Scenario 12.1: Both Players Click "Play Again"
- **Given** The game has ended; both players see the Game Over modal with "Play Again"
- **When** Player 1 clicks "Play Again"
- **And** Player 2 clicks "Play Again"
- **Then** Both players transition to a fresh Placement phase
- **Then** Both players' boards are empty (no ships placed)
- **Then** The room code remains the same
- **Then** Both players can place their 5 ships again
- **[AC-13]**

### Scenario 12.2: Play Again Waits for Both Players
- **Given** The game has ended
- **When** Only Player 1 clicks "Play Again"
- **Then** Player 1 sees a waiting state (e.g., "Waiting for opponent...")
- **Then** Player 2 still sees the Game Over modal with "Play Again"
- **When** Player 2 clicks "Play Again"
- **Then** Both players transition to the Placement phase
- **[AC-13]**
